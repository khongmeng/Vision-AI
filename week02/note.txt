- Indexed Color 
	> change image of 60 millions color into 256 colors. human eye can't even tell the difference
	> concept is to group a range of color into a single color (bin).
		+ create an indexed image and a indexed map
		+ when training, AI model doesn't need to know about indexed map
	> this somehow does auto-labeling too.

- Dithering
	> conveert grayscale image to Black&White, dithering will help in quality
	> For visualization, Dithering is good but for training CNN, Dithering will just introduce noise, so don't use it.
	> most of function call usually by default has dithering on, we need to turn off for training
